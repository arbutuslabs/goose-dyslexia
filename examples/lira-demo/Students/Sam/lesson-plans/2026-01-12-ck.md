# Lesson plan excerpt (illustrative, abbreviated)

Student: S.  Objective: ck says /k/ after a short vowel (sound only).  Previous concept: th.

## Previous errors

- reading: thin → "fin"
- spelling: ship → "sip"

## Auditory drill

Error sounds
- /th/: th
- /sh/: sh

Review sounds
- /ch/: ch
- /k/: c, k
- /a/: a
- /o/: o

## Blending (nonsense words)

- th vs f: thep / fep
- sh vs s: shif / sif
- previous concept, th: thab, thum
- new concept, ck: dack, zeck, vick, yock, juck

## Reading words (ck)

back, sock, duck, kick, deck, pack, rock, neck, lock, tick

## Spelling words (ck)

sack, luck, pick, dock, peck, tuck, rack, lick

## Reading sentences

1. Did the buck run?
2. Hack the muck and nip the wick.
3. The puck is in the thick mud, and Beth can not get it.

## Sight words

Review: the, was, and
New (suggestion): of

## Oral reading: "Seth and the Shell"

Seth has a shed. In the shed is a thin mat. On the mat sit a shell, a dish, and a fish. Did Seth get the fish? Yes! Beth and Seth chat. Then Beth shuts the shed.
