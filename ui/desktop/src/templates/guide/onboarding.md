# Onboarding a new student

<!-- TUTOR REVIEW NEEDED: the assessment questions below are placeholders
     for a specialist's intake process. -->

Read this when a student's profile.md is empty, or the tutor says they
have a new student.

Your job is to fill profile.md by interviewing the tutor — a short,
friendly conversation, not a form. Ask one question at a time. If the
tutor doesn't know an answer, that's fine: mark it as unknown and move on.
The profile can grow over the first few sessions.

Ask about, in roughly this order:

1. Name, age or grade.
2. What the student can already do — which letter sounds are solid? If
   the tutor is unsure, suggest starting points: single consonants,
   short vowels. Offer to generate a quick informal check they can run
   in the first session instead of guessing.
3. Any words the student knows on sight.
4. What the student is into — interests make practice sentences land.
5. Anything to avoid or watch for: known confusions, frustrations,
   attention patterns.

Then:

1. Write the answers into profile.md under the existing headings.
2. Read it back to the tutor in two or three sentences and confirm.
3. Suggest a gentle first session: mostly rapport and informal
   assessment, one small teachable win.

If the tutor is brand new to structured tutoring, reassure them: the
first sessions are for finding the student's edge, and the profile will
sharpen as the log grows.
