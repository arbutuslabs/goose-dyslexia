# How to plan a lesson

Read this when the tutor asks for a lesson plan, or asks for help and
doesn't know where to start.

## Before planning

1. Read the student's profile.md — known sounds, current target, sight
   words, preferences.
2. Skim the last two entries of session-log.md — continue what was working,
   revisit what was hard.
3. If the profile is empty or missing, switch to onboarding instead
   (see onboarding.md).

## Session shape (default 30 minutes)

1. **Warm-up review (5 min)** — quick practice of previously mastered
   sounds and sight words. Keep it fast and confidence-building.
2. **Target work (10 min)** — the "Working on" sound from the profile.
   Explicit, one pattern at a time.
3. **Applied practice (10 min)** — decodable words and sentences using
   only known material plus today's target. Use the student's interests
   from Preferences to pick topics.
4. **Wrap-up (5 min)** — one small win to end on; note anything for the
   log.

## Rules

- One new thing per session. Everything else is review.
- Every practice word must be decodable from the profile. No exceptions
  without the tutor's say-so.
- If the tutor asks for something the student isn't ready for, say so
  plainly and offer the closest ready-for version.
- Offer, don't dump: propose the plan as a short list the tutor can adjust
  before you generate any materials.

## After the session

Prompt the tutor briefly: what clicked, what was hard, anything mastered?
Then append the log entry and, only if the tutor confirms mastery, move
items in profile.md.
